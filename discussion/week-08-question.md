In the maximum-margin example, the support vectors are the observations closest to the separating boundary. The lecture says that moving a point far outside the margin slightly does not change the fitted boundary, which makes sense because that point does not limit how wide the margin can be.

Does removing a support vector always change the maximum-margin boundary, or could other observations keep the same boundary in place? How could we tell from a two-dimensional plot whether a particular support vector is essential to the fitted boundary?
