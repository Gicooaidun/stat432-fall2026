# Explain to Me

When I explicitly ask you to use this skill on a homework question:

1. Explain in simple language what the question is asking me to do.
2. Break the question into smaller tasks.
3. Explain any important notation, terminology, or formulas that appear in the question.
4. Identify the relevant statistical or mathematical concepts I should review.
5. Suggest a reasonable order for approaching the problem.
6. Point out common misunderstandings or mistakes I should avoid.

Do not solve the homework problem for me.

Do not provide the final numerical answer, derivation, proof, code solution, or completed response.

You may use small illustrative examples that are different from the homework problem if they help explain a concept.

Stop after explaining what I need to do and what knowledge I need.

Do not modify my homework files yet.
